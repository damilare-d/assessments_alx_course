Nigerian Jollof Rice

A rich, smoky, and flavourful one-pot rice dish cooked in a seasoned tomato base. This is the classic party-style Jollof Rice — bold, deeply coloured, and absolutely beloved across West Africa.

Recipe Details

Prep Time: 20 minutes

Cook Time: 45 minutes

Servings: 4 people

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 4 medium tomatoes, blended
  • 2 red bell peppers (tatashe), blended
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers (atarodo), blended
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 bay leaves

Instructions

  1. Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, and scotch bonnet peppers together until smooth. Set aside.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and fry until they turn golden.
  3. Pour the blended pepper mix into the pot and stir well. Cook on medium heat for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring frequently, until the raw smell is gone and the sauce thickens and darkens.
  4. Add the chicken stock, curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, and salt. Stir everything together and bring to a boil.
  5. Wash the rice thoroughly, then add it to the pot. Stir once to combine, then cover tightly with aluminium foil under the lid to trap the steam.
  6. Reduce the heat to low and cook for 25 to 30 minutes without lifting the lid, until the rice is fully cooked and has absorbed the sauce.
  7. Remove the bay leaves, fluff gently with a fork, and serve hot with fried chicken or plantain.

Tips & Notes

  • The smoky bottom crust (called "party Jollof" flavour) is intentional — let the rice sit on low heat for the last 5 minutes uncovered to get it.
  • Parboiled rice works best; avoid using regular white rice as it turns mushy.
  • For extra depth, use a mix of tomato paste and fresh blended tomatoes.
